# Quick Reference — Tomorrow's Meeting

**Read on phone 5 min before walking in. Don't memorise. Internalise.**

---

## OPEN (5 min)

Warmth. How are the kids / business / etc. Then:

> *"Before I tell you what I've been building, can you walk me through last week — what ate the most hours?"*

Listen. Count to 4 silently after she answers.

---

## DISCOVER (15 min) — Mom Test anchor questions

**Q1.** *"Of the three pains you mentioned — overcapacity, slow-paying clients, the phone calls — which one cost you the most money this quarter?"*

**Q2.** *"Show me your AR right now — what's outstanding over 60 days?"* (Get her to open Xero.)

**Q3.** *"Last time a client paid 90+ days late — walk me through what you did. Name the client if you can."*

**Q4.** *"What have you already tried to fix this? Tools, hires, processes — what stuck, what didn't, why?"*

**Q5.** *"If I disappeared tomorrow, what would you do about [her #1 pain] in the next 30 days?"*

After EACH answer: count to 4. Don't fill the silence.

---

## PITCH (3 min) — only after Q5

> *"I'm building a subscription product for accountants — automated AR recovery, around A$199-299/mo when it launches. I'll install it manually for you first, free for 90 days, in exchange for case study rights + 2 referrals. After 90 days, if it's working, you keep using it at the subscription rate. What I learn from your install becomes the productised version other accountants can use without me. You'd be the first."*

Pull out the **AR-Recovery Pilot Proposal one-pager** on your phone. Walk her through it.

---

## TEST FOR REAL (5 min) — three traps

**Trap 1 — she says yes too fast:**
> *"Before you say yes — this needs ~30 min/week from you for 90 days, and a 5-min video at the end. Still in?"*

**Trap 2 — she picks EOFY because it sounds important:**
> *"When did EOFY chasing last cost you a client or a dollar?"*
If she can't name a specific instance — pivot to AR-chase (her real bleed).

**Trap 3 — vague "2 referrals":**
> *"Let's name them now — who are the 2 accountants in your network I'd most want to meet? Names, not introductions yet."*

---

## CLOSE (5 min) — commitment escalator

**Small (right now):**
> *"Can you export your aged AR report right now? Even a screenshot from Xero is fine."*

If she won't do this today — pilot is dead. Politely close, follow up in 30 days.

**Medium (this week):**
> *"Block 60 min next [Tue/Wed/Thu] — I'll walk you through the build plan and we sign a 1-page pilot agreement."*

**Large (before you leave):**
> *"Last thing — name 5 accountants I should talk to for market understanding. Names, not introductions yet."*

Write each name in your phone before walking out.

---

## SCORE CHECK

- ✅ She gave a dollar figure (Q2 or Q3) — 2 pts
- ✅ She named a recent specific late payer (Q3) — 2 pts
- ✅ She sent the AR export — 2 pts
- ✅ She named 5 accountants — 2 pts
- ✅ Kickoff time booked in her calendar — 2 pts

**10/10 = real pilot. Build starts this week.**
**6-8/10 = warm but soft. Follow up in 7 days.**
**Below 6 = polite-friend, not buyer. Park, move to next prospect.**

---

## DON'T

- Don't pitch before Q5
- Don't fill her silence
- Don't promise specific recovery numbers
- Don't mention AI receptionist (different product, different conversation)
- Don't say "AI agency" — say "subscription product / automated AR recovery"
- Don't propose A$199/mo as a today-payment — pilot is free

---

## AFTER THE MEETING (within 2 hours)

1. Email her the AR-Recovery Pilot Proposal PDF
2. Send the kickoff calendar invite (use the slot you locked in the meeting)
3. Text me her answers to Q1-Q5 + score + names

Then we design the n8n + Brevo + Xero workflow from her real data.
