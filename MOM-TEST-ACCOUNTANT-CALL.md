## 1. The 3 Rules → 1 Question Each

**Rule 1 — Her life, not your idea:**
*"Walk me through last week — what ate the most hours?"*

**Rule 2 — Past specifics, not future opinions:**
*"Last BAS quarter — how many clients filed late, and what did you actually do about it?"*

**Rule 3 — Talk less, listen more:**
After every answer, count to 3 silently. Then ask: *"What happened next?"*

---

## 2. Five Anchor Questions

**Q1.** *"Of the three pains you told me about — overcapacity, slow-paying clients, the phone calls — which one cost you the most money this quarter?"*
- **Signal:** She names a dollar figure or specific clients.
- **Failure:** *"They're all bad."* Push: *"Which one would you fix first if you could only pick one?"*

**Q2.** *"Show me your AR right now — what's outstanding over 60 days?"*
- **Signal:** She opens Xero / quotes a real number.
- **Failure:** Vague *"a fair bit."* Push: *"Roughly — five grand? Fifty?"*

**Q3.** *"Last time a client paid 90+ days late — walk me through what you did. Emails, calls, who chased?"*
- **Signal:** Concrete sequence + who did the work + time spent.
- **Failure:** *"We usually just call them."* Push: *"Tell me about the most recent one. Name it."*

**Q4.** *"What have you already tried to fix this? Tools, hires, processes — what stuck, what didn't, and why?"*
- **Signal:** Real attempts + real reasons they failed = real budget appetite.
- **Failure:** *"Nothing really."* That means low pain. Re-test Q1.

**Q5.** *"If I disappeared tomorrow, what would you do about [her #1 pain] in the next 30 days?"*
- **Signal:** She has a plan / budget / a person already in mind.
- **Failure:** *"Just keep going."* → pain is tolerable. Pilot will drift.

---

## 3. Three Traps

**Trap 1 — She says yes too fast.**
Reply: *"Before you say yes — this needs ~30 min/week from you for 90 days, and a 5-min video at the end. Still in?"* If she flinches, it wasn't real.

**Trap 2 — She picks EOFY because it sounds important.**
Test: *"When did EOFY chasing last cost you a client or a dollar?"* If she can't name one, switch lead-in to AR-chase. Slow payment is bleeding now; EOFY pain is seasonal memory.

**Trap 3 — Vague "2 referrals if it works."**
Reply: *"Let's name them now — who are the 2 accountants in your network I'd most want to meet? I won't contact them yet. Just names."* Names on paper = real commitment. *"I'll think about it"* = zombie.

## 4. Commitment Escalator

**Small (during call):**
*"Send me your aged receivables report and last quarter's BAS client list today — I'll scope the AR workflow tonight."*

**Medium (end of call):**
*"Block 90 min next Tuesday — I'll walk you through the build plan and we sign a 1-page pilot agreement."*

**Large (Tuesday session):**
*"Pilot starts June 1. $500 token, 90 days, AR-chase workflow. In return: written case study, 5-min video at day 90, and the 2 names you gave me — warm intros by day 100. Sign here."*

If she won't do the small one today, the pilot is dead — politely close and move on.
