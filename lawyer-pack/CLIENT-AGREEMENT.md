## Metis is not a lawyer, and this is not legal advice

Metis is a tool for organising your paperwork. It is not a law firm, it is not a solicitor, and nothing it shows you is legal advice. It can tell you what documents a matter like yours usually needs and help you keep track of them. It cannot tell you what to argue, what you are entitled to, or what will happen in your case.

## A solicitor has the final say

Anything Metis produces — a checklist, a summary, a matter pack — is a starting point for a conversation with a qualified Australian solicitor. It is not a legal document and it has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Do not file it, send it, sign it, or rely on it in your matter until a solicitor has looked at it and told you it is right.

## Metis can be wrong

Metis uses AI, and AI makes mistakes. It cannot read handwriting or scanned documents — when that happens it will tell you so rather than pretend. When it suggests that a file you uploaded matches something on your checklist, that is a guess for you to confirm. Nothing is ever ticked off unless you say so. Please check anything that matters.

## If your situation is urgent, get help now — not from Metis

Metis is not for emergencies. If you or a child are in immediate danger, call 000. If you are worried about family violence, or that someone may take a child overseas or refuse to return them, that needs a lawyer urgently — there are court orders that can only help if they are made in time. Free help: Legal Aid NSW LawAccess 1300 888 529, or 1800RESPECT (1800 737 732) for family violence support. Do not wait on Metis before making those calls.

## If your matter goes to court, there are rules about using AI

The Federal Circuit and Family Court issued a Practice Direction on artificial intelligence on 29 May 2026, and it applies to self-represented people, not just lawyers. In short: you stay responsible for anything you file, and anything AI helped produce has to be checked before it goes to the court — legal references especially. People have been penalised for filing AI-invented case citations. Metis deliberately will not give you case citations for this reason. If your matter is heading to court, tell your solicitor you have used a tool like this.

## Your information

What you upload is stored so the tool can work, and is visible only to your account. We do not use your documents or your matter to train AI models. To read and answer your questions, the text of your documents is sent to Anthropic in the United States, under commercial terms that prohibit training on it. If that is not acceptable for your matter, please don't upload sensitive material — and raise it with your solicitor. Using Metis does not create a lawyer–client relationship, and what you write here is not privileged; if privilege matters to your case, ask your solicitor before uploading.

## This is early software

Metis is new and still being built. Parts of it will be rough, and some things will be missing entirely. We don't promise it is complete or error-free — please check anything that matters. Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law are not affected. You can stop at any time and ask us to delete your matter.
