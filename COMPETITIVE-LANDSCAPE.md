# Metis Cortex — AU AI Receptionist Competitive Landscape

**Compiled:** 2026-05-08
**For:** Peter Kritsotakis, sales-call ammunition
**Method:** Live web research (vendor sites, Capterra, Tracxn, PitchBook, Startup Daily, comparison reviews). Where a price wasn't on the live page at search time, it's marked **Unverified — check at sales call.**

---

## 1. TL;DR

The AU AI receptionist market is crowded but bifurcated: at the bottom is a price-led self-serve tier ($48–$149/mo, no setup, no hand-holding) where churn is high because tradies can't configure prompts; at the top is a managed tier ($600–$1,299/mo + $990–$3,500 setup) where Sophiie, Valory and LANA actually compete with Metis Cortex on done-for-you delivery. **The real threats are Sophiie AI (175+ businesses, ~$1.4M ARR, bootstrapped, trades-heavy) and Chime Labs (Sydney, $900K pre-seed from 500 Global, ex-Google founders, tradie-only).** Everyone else is either too cheap to deliver or too generic to vertical-fit. **What Peter says on calls:** "Cheaper tools exist — they make you do the prompt engineering. Sophiie and Chime are real, but neither guarantees install in 14 days, neither refunds you if you don't save 10 hours, and neither locks $800/mo for life. I'm an operator who built five businesses — I'm not selling you software, I'm selling you the install."

## 3. Threat-by-Threat Analysis (Top 5)

### 3.1 Sophiie AI — the real ceiling competitor
Founded 2024, Surfers Paradise. Bootstrapped to $1.4M ARR in 2025 with ~30 staff. CEO Jacob Banks + co-founder Luke Kelleher. 175+ business customers, claims 67,000+ calls handled. Targets exactly Metis Cortex's ICP (trades, medical, legal, hospitality, professional services) and integrates with ServiceM8, Xero, Google Calendar, Outlook. Positions as "Australia's first purpose-built AI office manager" and pitches as a *full admin replacement*, not just an answering service. Pricing ~$300/mo + ~$800 setup (discounted to ~$400 via Tradiespace partnership). **What they do well:** brand presence, channel partner with Tradiespace, broad vertical coverage, story of bootstrapped AU growth. **Where they break:** no published guarantee, setup fee is real but smaller than ours (looks "cheaper" on first glance), they market broad — if your prospect is a 1-2 location boutique restaurant or a real-estate agency, Sophiie's positioning is generic trades-heavy. **Would they win our deal?** Yes — for tradies on ServiceM8, often. No — for restaurants, real estate, beauty/aesthetic where their case studies are thin and the operator-to-operator pitch lands harder.

### 3.2 Chime Labs — the funded threat
Sydney, founded 2025. $900K AUD pre-seed led by 500 Global, ex-Google founders (Alexis Griveau CEO + Mathew Pretel). Tradie-only positioning right now, with stated roadmap into quoting, invoicing and lead generation. Pricing not published on their pricing page at scrape time — **Unverified — check at sales call.** **What they do well:** investor backing, clean technical pedigree, sharp ICP focus. **Where they break:** young, narrow vertical, no guarantees published, founder profile is engineer-led (not operator-led), unproven on integrations beyond marketing claims. **Would they win our deal?** For sophisticated tradies who care about "ex-Google + 500 Global pedigree," yes. For operators who want a peer who's actually run a service business through quiet seasons, no — that's our terrain.

### 3.3 Johnni AI — the integrations specialist
Adelaide-built, deeply integrated into ServiceM8, Simpro, ServiceTitan, Aroflo, plus HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho, Calendly, Shopify, Xero, Stripe, Mailchimp. AU voices trained on "thousands of Australian calls," understands trade slang ("HWS," "RCD trip," "rego"). Headline price $99/mo with a 30-day free trial; realistic pricing $300–$600+ with setup. **What they do well:** the integration story is the strongest in the market for tradies. 30-day free trial is genuinely longer than most. **Where they break:** outside trades the offer thins (real estate, hair/beauty pages exist but feel thin). No stacked guarantees. Self-serve trial means buyer carries config burden during the 30 days — which is exactly when most tradies give up. **Would they win our deal?** For a Simpro-shop electrician with a tech-curious owner — yes. For a restaurant owner or a clinic manager who wants someone to *do it for them* — no.

### 3.4 Valory AI — the closest structural twin
Done-for-you, AU-hosted, Australian-accented voices, pitched at clinics, gyms, trades, accounting. Pricing $149–$1,299/mo + $990 setup (waived on 3-month commitment). Claim: dedicated success lead, escalation logic, call review. **What they do well:** their structure looks almost identical to ours from the outside — managed, AU, vertical-aware. **Where they break:** no public guarantee on missed calls handled or hours saved, no install timeline commitment, and they waive setup only on a 3-month commit (we waive on 14-day install miss — a far stronger trigger). **Would they win our deal?** This is the deal we lose if we don't lead with the two stacked guarantees + the operator story. Without them, Valory looks identical and slightly cheaper.

### 3.5 LANA Software — the JMS-deep tradie play
Three tiers: $300/mo (basic AI receptionist) + $1,000 setup, $399/mo (qualifies + creates job in JMS) + $1,500 setup, $600/mo (full CRM + AI + JMS) + $3,500 setup. Trade-only ICP with deep simPRO/ServiceM8/Ascora/OpenSolar/AroFlo integration. **What they do well:** the $600/$3,500 tier is genuinely close to a full ops platform. **Where they break:** $3,500 setup is the highest in the market and they don't waive it on missed install. No performance guarantee. Pure trades — they'll never win a clinic, restaurant or real-estate deal. **Would they win our deal?** Only on a high-end tradie with budget who specifically wants quote/invoice chasing built in. Otherwise no.

## 5. Buyer Journey Reality Check — Pain Points → Metis Cortex Mapping

From dev.to "the part of building an AI receptionist nobody talks about," Yes AI's "4 of 7 vendors failed within 30 days" finding, and recurring complaints across comparison reviews:

| Buyer pain point reported | Metis Cortex answer |
|---|---|
| AI confirms a booking that never landed in the calendar (CRM API timeout, agent says "you're booked," customer shows up to nothing) | Done-for-you 14-day install includes calendar write-verification before go-live; we don't ship a deployment until the booking round-trip is tested. Refund trigger: 10 hours saved or refund forces us to fix this fast. |
| Voice sounds American / robotic / wrong cadence for AU callers | AU operator-led install, AU voice selection, AU phone number provisioning. Operator-to-operator brand voice (we pick voices that match how a Narrabeen restaurant would actually answer). |
| Self-serve trial: tradie quits in week 2 because they can't configure prompts | Done-for-you install, not a free trial. The setup fee buys our time, not the right to figure it out yourself. |
| US data residency / privacy concerns for medical and legal | AU operator (data residency claim must be verified in our infra; flag for ops if not yet AU-hosted). |
| Silent failure — bookings drop 15% before anyone notices | We deploy with monitoring + weekly review during founding-client period. (If we don't currently do this, build it before next sales call.) |
| Setup fee that doesn't get waived even when install is late | Our 14-day install guarantee waives the setup fee. |
| No performance guarantee anywhere in the contract | 100% missed calls handled + 10hrs saved or refund. Written. |
| Lock-in contracts (Smith.ai 6-12 mo, Numa annual, Replicant annual) | Founding rate is "locked for life" — buyer benefit, not a buyer obligation. No lock-in equivalent on prospect side. |
| Generic vertical fit — "tradie tool used by a restaurant" | One slot per vertical at the founding rate. Forces our vertical specialisation and gives the prospect exclusivity. |

## 6. Sales-Call Ammunition — 5 Lines

1. **"Yes there are cheaper options. Here's what they break on."** — TransferToAI is $99 and Hooroo starts at $75. Both are self-serve. The cost isn't the monthly fee, it's the 30 hours of prompt-tuning you'll do in the first month. Yes AI tested 7 vendors and 4 failed in 30 days. The failure mode is always the same: install never finishes.

2. **"I tried [Sophiie / Johnni]. Three things I noticed."** — One: the trial is on you to configure, not them. Two: no published refund if it doesn't work. Three: the integration claims look great in marketing — ask them to show you a live booking round-trip on your CRM before you sign. Most can't.

3. **"Two guarantees, both written, both refundable."** — 100% missed calls handled in month one, or refund. 10 hours saved in month one, or refund. Install in 14 days, or setup fee is waived. Three triggers, three refunds. Ask any other vendor for that in writing — they won't write it.

4. **"Founding rate is one slot per vertical."** — I take five clients at $800/mo locked for life. One restaurant, one boutique real-estate agency, one medical/dental, one beauty/aesthetic, one cleaning/trades. I'm an operator first — I run DSK, Eonia, HydraLab, Limani Seafood. I'm picking case studies, not chasing logos.

5. **"I'm not VC-funded. That's the feature."** — Chime raised $900K from 500 Global. Sophiie's bootstrapped but scaling. Both will pivot if growth slows. I'm self-funded by five operating businesses. The founding rate at $800/mo is locked for life because I don't have a board telling me to raise it next round.

## 7. What NOT to Do — Competitor Moves We Should Avoid

- **Don't drop to a $99 self-serve tier.** TransferToAI owns it, and the buyer at that price is a budget-shopper who will churn. Ceding price floor saves nothing.
- **Don't lead with integration count.** Johnni lists 20+ integrations on the homepage. We lead with the install promise, not the integration list. Integrations are table stakes; the install is the moat.
- **Don't write a 6 or 12-month lock-in.** Smith.ai does this and loses AU SMB deals on it. Our founding-rate "for life" is the inverse — a *prospect* benefit.
- **Don't market to "all SMBs."** Sophiie targets everyone and Valory targets clinics+gyms+trades+accounting — both look generic. One slot per vertical forces us to look intentional.
- **Don't bury the guarantee in fine print.** Smith.ai's 30-day refund caps at $1,000 and excludes overages. Ours has no cap and no exclusions — write it that way and lead with it.
- **Don't pitch "AI office manager" / "full admin replacement"** (Sophiie's lane). It overcommits and invites the prospect to compare us to a $65K human. Pitch *missed-call recovery + booking* — narrow, demonstrable, refundable.
- **Don't waive setup for a 3-month commit (Valory's model).** Waive setup for *us* missing the install date. Triggers on our performance, not their commitment.
