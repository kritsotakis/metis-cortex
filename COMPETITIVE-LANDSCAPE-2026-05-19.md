## 2. Where Metis CAN'T win head-on (be honest)

1. **SMB self-serve at A$49–$300/mo** — Trillet, CallMate, Sophiie own this. Price-war is unwinnable; the buyer who chooses A$49 is structurally not Metis's buyer.
2. **GP clinics** — Healthengine's distribution + Best Practice/Pracsoft integration is a moat Metis cannot cross without a Healthengine partnership. Don't bother.
3. **Tradies on ServiceM8/Simpro broadly** — Sophiie + Johnni + LANA + Chime are all targeting this with deeper integration and lower price. Metis can win specific Sydney trades deals on the operator pitch, but **cannot own the vertical**.
4. **Enterprise compliance** — AiDial is positioned for this and will out-credential a 1-person agency.

## 3. Where Metis CAN win — 3 angles, ranked by defensibility

**Angle A — Done-for-you install for compliance-heavy SME verticals (DEFENSIBLE).** Medical aesthetic (AHPRA), dental cosmetic, allied health day-clinics, boutique law/accounting. These verticals have: (a) high call-failure cost, (b) AHPRA/Privacy Act exposure that scares them off A$49 self-serve, (c) no Healthengine distribution moat (Helen is GP-only), (d) buyers who pay A$1,200/mo without flinching. **This is the lane.** Metis becomes "the done-for-you AI receptionist that won't get you struck off."

**Angle B — Sydney-trades operator pitch with one-slot-per-vertical lock-in (DEFENSIBLE for ~6 months).** The "founder ran DSK + Eonia + HydraLab + Limani" story is genuinely differentiated vs Sophiie/Chime engineer-founders. But this lane decays — Chime will raise Series A in 12 months and out-spend the story. Treat as **wedge into Angle A**, not a destination.

**Angle C — Multilingual (Greek/Mandarin/Vietnamese) for Sydney suburb-specific trades (UNTESTED).** Nobody is doing this. Sydney trades demographics support it. Risk: voice quality in non-English is unproven on Retell/ElevenLabs at production. **Test before betting.**

## 4. Honest verdict — is AI receptionist the right market for Metis right now?

**No, not as the flagship.** The window where a horizontal AU AI receptionist could land closed between May 8 and May 19 — Healthengine Helen ate GP, Trillet + CallMate collapsed the floor to A$49, and Sophiie shipped multi-channel inbox + Android. A new entrant at A$2,500 + A$750/mo (the proposed reprice) gets pattern-matched to Valory and loses on volume.

**Three paths forward, ranked:**

1. **Re-position to "done-for-you AI workflow automation for compliance-heavy SME verticals"** — AI receptionist becomes ONE module inside a wider AR-chase + intake + booking + follow-up stack. Sells at A$2,500–$5,000 setup + A$750–$1,500/mo without competing head-on with A$49 floor.
2. **Pick ONE vertical (aesthetic clinics OR boutique law) and own it end-to-end** — receptionist + intake + AHPRA-safe marketing automation + lapsed-client recall. Three competitors max in that lane (MedReception, Solium, Crown).
3. **Stay generic AI receptionist** — only viable if Peter accepts a 12–18 month grind to 30 customers against four better-funded incumbents. Don't recommend.

**Pick 1 or 2 this week. Don't ship the A$2,500/$750 reprice until vertical is chosen** — that price needs vertical justification or it reads as undifferentiated managed-tier.
