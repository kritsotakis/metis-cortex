# Scope Discovery — Capture This If She Says Yes

**For Peter to fill in (or get on the spot) the moment she commits to the pilot.**

Don't leave the meeting without at least the bolded items. Everything else can come in the 60-min kickoff call within 14 days.

---

## 1. Get her aged AR export TODAY

**🚨 Most important single item.**

Ask: *"Can you export your aged receivables report right now? Even a screenshot from Xero is fine — I just need to see what we're working with."*

What you're looking for:
- Total A$ outstanding
- A$ over 30 days
- A$ over 60 days
- A$ over 90 days
- Count of invoices in each bucket
- Top 5 oldest invoices (amount + age + client name)

If she won't export it on the spot — pilot is dead, the commitment isn't real.

---

## 2. Software stack

- **Primary accounting software:** Xero / MYOB / QuickBooks / other?
- **Practice management:** Karbon / FYI / XPM / Practice Ignition / Ignition / Karbon / none?
- **Email tool:** Outlook / Gmail / Mailbird / their PM's built-in email?
- **SMS tool (if any):** ClickSend / their accounting software's built-in / none?
- **Payment processor:** Stripe / EzyCollect / IntegraPay / direct deposit only?
- **Client portal:** Karbon Client Portal / FYI / SuiteFiles / none?

---

## 3. Tone + voice

Ask: *"If I sent you three sample chase emails — one formal, one warm-professional, one direct — which sounds most like how your firm talks to clients?"*

Then ask: *"Are there clients who want you to be MORE direct than that? Or MORE polite? Any segmentation rules?"*

Goal: capture her firm's chase voice. Get 1-2 examples of chase emails her team has actually sent (best signal).

---

## 4. Client list + segmentation

- **Roughly how many active clients?** (10s, 100s, 1000s?)
- **How are clients categorised?** Industry / size / how-long-with-firm / fee tier?
- **VIP / "do not automate" list:** which clients should NEVER receive automated chase emails? (Family, friends, slow-but-reliable, sensitive negotiations in progress)
- **Recent payment-plan clients:** anyone currently on a payment arrangement we should exclude?

---

## 5. Current chase process (what we're replacing/augmenting)

- Who currently chases overdue invoices? (Senior accountant / junior staff / bookkeeper / her personally?)
- How often? (Weekly / monthly / when she remembers?)
- Approximate hours/week spent on AR chase today?
- What's the LAST cycle look like? Walk her through one specific recent example.
- What's stopped working / what's the breaking point?

---

## 6. The "what does success look like" question

Ask directly: *"At day 90, what number on your aged AR report would make you say 'this worked'?"*

Listen for:
- Reduction in 60+ day buckets
- Dollar amount recovered
- Hours/week saved
- Reduction in client-paying disputes
- "Don't have to think about it anymore"

Her answer = your KPI = the case study headline.

---

## 7. Sensitivities + non-negotiables

- Are there any client segments where automated comms are RISKY (eg. SMSF clients, audit clients in dispute)?
- Are there TPB / regulatory concerns about how chase emails are phrased?
- Does the firm have a privacy policy you need to align with?
- Anything she'd want you to NEVER mention in a chase email?

---

## 8. Schedule the kickoff

Get a 60-minute Zoom or in-person session in her diary within 14 days. **Don't leave without a confirmed time slot.**

If she gives "I'll get back to you with dates" — push gently: *"I can hold three slots — Tuesday 10am, Thursday 2pm, or Friday 9am. Pick one and I'll send the calendar invite now."*

---

## 9. The two committed referral names (per ChatGPT)

Before you leave:

*"Last thing — can you name 2-3 other accountants in your network I should talk to? Not for referrals yet — just for market understanding. I want to know if other firms have the same AR pain you do. Names only, not introductions."*

Write the names in your phone before walking out. Even one or two names = real commitment.
