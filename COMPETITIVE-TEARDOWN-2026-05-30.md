## The headline finding

The "no one does this" claim from the 2026-05-28 LEAP/Smokeball deep-dive scope was too narrow. **Law Brief AI is doing the core motion now** (live face-to-face + VoIP capture → file note + client letter, beside Smokeball/LEAP, AU-hosted, AU family-law firm deployed). **AI Legal Assistant** is adjacent (upload-only, but family-law-dedicated + SOC 2 Type 2 + ISO 27001:2022). The defensible Metis Cortex wedge narrows to **four things none of them have together**:

1. **Real-time AustLII case-law authority lookup *during* the conference** (not from training data, not post-hoc)
2. **Family-law-specific cognition tuned to post-2024 parenting + post-June-2025 codified four-step property reforms** (current-law discipline)
3. **All-party-consent workflow + privilege architecture built for NSW practice** (audit-trail-grade, separately stored, defensible — vs. competitors who are silent on consent)
4. **Case brief + client proposal** (Law Brief AI = file note + client letter only; Smokeball Archie + LEAP Matter AI = reason over already-typed matter content; AI Legal Assistant = upload-based meeting minutes)

## Where each competitor beats us today (acknowledge, don't paper over)

### Law Brief AI
- **Already deployed in a "leading Sunshine Coast family-law firm"** — unnamed but specific
- **Quantified traction:** 200+ consultations processed, 400+ documents generated, 50+ hours saved per month
- **90-day money-back guarantee** ("30+ hours/mo saved by end of month three or refund of month three")
- **VoIP phone-call auto-capture** — we don't have this yet, and phone consults are >50% of solo-practitioner conferences
- **Pricing anchor:** "less than two billable hours a month" + "$72K/mo productivity recovery" example — concrete

### AI Legal Assistant
- **SOC 2 Type 2 + ISO 27001:2022** — actual certifications, not roadmap. Big for risk-averse buyers
- **Already in Smokeball marketplace + Clio + ActionStep integrations live**
- **Dedicated family-law landing page** with Lighthouse compliance, parenting plans, property settlements, DV screening references
- **7 named individual testimonials** with role labels (Director, Senior Partner, Senior Lawyer, Conveyancer, etc.)

### Smokeball Archie
- **Native to the practice-management system** — solicitor doesn't add a vendor, they use a feature they already pay for
- **Audio transcription (uploaded files, 1GB) + voice dictation** — incumbent feature parity is a single sprint away from live recording
- **A$49-239/user/month** baseline already paid by ~majority of small-firm AU family-law practitioners

### LEAP Matter AI
- **A$20M+ annual R&D spend** — they will close any feature gap that proves commercially meaningful within ~12 months
- **3-year contracts** — buyers already locked in
- **Native trust accounting, billing, FCFCOA forms** — irreplaceable, not our market

## Where Metis Cortex actually wins (the 4 UNIQUE rows, defended)

### 1. Real-time AustLII authority lookup *during* the conference

| Reviewer signal | Verification |
|---|---|
| ChatGPT: "Family-law cognition is not commodity… generic recording is" | None of the 4 competitors search case law in real-time during the conference. Smokeball Archie reasons over typed matter content. Law Brief AI doesn't mention case law at all. AI Legal Assistant offers transcription + meeting minutes, no case authority. LEAP Matter AI works over existing matter. |
| Manus: "AustLII integration during the conference (not post-processing) is the most technically distinctive feature" | Verified — Law Brief AI homepage features explicitly do not include any case-law / authority lookup. AI Legal Assistant family-law page lists "Lighthouse compliance" / "parenting plans" but no AustLII integration. |

**Mom-Test sentence to land:** *"When the client tells you something about a property dispute, the right Family Law Act provision and the on-point case are on the page during the conversation. Not in an email at 9pm. Law Brief AI captures the conversation but doesn't search the law. Smokeball Archie reasons over what's already typed into the matter — not what's being said."*

### 2. Family-law cognition tuned to current reforms

| Source | Detail |
|---|---|
| Legal knowledge base updated 2026-05-29 (commit 6910a66) | Removed repealed s61DA ESPR + s65DAA equal-time + Goode v Goode framing; added post-2024 s60CC + post-June-2025 codified 4-step + s71B/s90RI disclosure + s4AB family-violence definition |
| Site copy now references reforms | Updated this session: "deeply tuned to the post-2024 parenting framework and the post-June-2025 codified property four-step" |

**Defensibility window:** 12-18 months. Generic AI tools will eventually train on the reforms; the sustainable layer is the AustLII-live + consent-workflow combination, not the knowledge base alone. (Manus warning held.)

**Mom-Test sentence to land:** *"Generic AI tools will still cite Goode v Goode — repealed in 2024. We rebuilt the family-law layer for the current law."*

### 3. All-party-consent workflow + privilege architecture

| Source | Detail |
|---|---|
| Gemini | NSW *Surveillance Devices Act 2007* s7(1): all-party consent for private conversations, 5yr / 500 penalty units |
| Manus | Site is silent on consent in "How it works" — "single most likely demo-killer omission" |
| Cowork | NSW Supreme Court Practice Note SC Gen 23 + ASCR r.9.1 + Lawcover PI = 4-vector exposure |
| Site (updated today) | New Step 1 explicitly addresses consent capture, timestamping, separate storage |

**Verified on competitor pages:** Law Brief AI homepage says nothing about consent management or workflow. AI Legal Assistant doesn't address it. Smokeball Archie audio-transcription support article = nothing. **This is currently the only competitor surface explicitly addressing it — but only because we wrote it today.** Once Law Brief AI sees it land, they can add a consent step within a sprint. So the moat here isn't the *existence* of the workflow; it's the depth (audit trail + privilege analysis + Lawcover-engagement posture) that we build into it.

**Mom-Test sentence to land:** *"Recording client conferences in NSW is a Surveillance Devices Act question, not a tech question. We made the consent workflow the first step of every matter and built the audit trail to prove it. Have you had the privilege conversation with your insurer yet?"*
