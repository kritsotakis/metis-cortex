## Whichever variant you use

**Always include in any follow-up:**
- AR-Recovery Pilot Proposal PDF attached (one-pager)
- Your phone number in signature
- Specific next step (book / send AR / honest reply) — never end with "let me know"

**Always do within 2 hours:**
- Send the email
- Text me how it went (Q1-Q5 answers + score + 5 names + commitment level)
- Block any follow-up calendar slots she agreed to (real action, not hope)

**Never do:**
- Wait 48 hours then send a hopeful "just checking in" — looks desperate, signals weak demand
- Ask for the case study / referrals BEFORE the pilot has produced results
- Negotiate price down to keep her ("but for you, I could do A$99/mo") — kills your product economics
