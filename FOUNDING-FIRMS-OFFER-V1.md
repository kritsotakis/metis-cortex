## The scarcity is real and the reason-why is visible

**Five firms. Five only.**

Why five? Each founding firm gets ~10 hours per month of Peter personally — across onboarding (sitting in the first 3 conferences), weekly calls (30 min × 12 weeks), and joint Lawcover engagement. That's ~50 hours/month at five firms. Sixth firm = diluting your share of attention. Honest constraint, not a marketing constraint.

**Two ways the window closes:**
1. The fifth founding firm signs.
2. The first founding firm publishes a public case study — at that point the offer changes (price likely rises, bonus stack shrinks, the early-firm advantage diminishes).

**Standard rate post-cohort = 71% higher.** A founding firm saves ~A$30,000 per solicitor over 5 years vs the same firm signing in month 13. Real money. Real reason to be first.

## What kind of firm we'd say no to (also real)

Not everyone is the right fit. We'd genuinely turn down:

- **Firms wanting 3-year contracts** — we won't sign one. If your procurement insists on it, we're not aligned.
- **Firms whose IT requires SOC 2 certification today** — we don't have it yet (it's the Year-1 build goal). AI Legal Assistant has it. They're better-fit for you right now; come back to us in 9 months.
- **Firms with zero family-law caseload** — Metis is family-law-deep first. We add other practice areas in Year 2-3. If you're 90% commercial litigation, we're the wrong tool today.
- **Firms wanting white-label or "Konstan Lawyers by Metis Cortex"** — we run one brand. The matter outputs use your letterhead with an "AI assistance" disclosure footer; the platform stays Metis Cortex.

Saying no honestly to firms we can't serve well is part of why founding firms get the time and attention they pay for.

## What the 15-minute first conversation actually is

**It is not a demo.**

It is a Mom-Test conversation about your conference workflow:

- Walk me through the last new-client conference you ran. When did you actually write the file note? How long did it take?
- When's the last time you were doing notes after hours? What was that like?
- Has a client ever come back later and said "you never told me that" — tell me what happened.
- What practice management software are you on? When did it last frustrate you?
- If a prospect arrived for a first consult with their documents pre-organised, chronology already in their head, asset positions roughly mapped — would that be a help or a threat?

**No pitch. No slides. No card.** If we're a fit, you get a free 90-day pilot. If we're not, you get my honest opinion on which of the five tools in the AU family-law space is the right fit for you instead.

The first 15 minutes is research. The proof is in the work.
