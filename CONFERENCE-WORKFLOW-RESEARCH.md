# Metis Cortex — AU Solicitor Client-Conference Workflow (research-verified)

**Purpose:** the canonical, source-verified map of how an Australian solicitor–client conference actually runs and what must be captured/produced — so the product's transcription → file note / case brief / proposal / costs templates miss nothing, and the Mom-Test asks the right things. Researched 2026-05-29 (Code, 2 deep-research agents, URL-cited). **This should have been done before building the templates — see lesson in memory.**

> ⚠️ **Currency flags — do NOT encode pre-reform framing:**
> - **Parenting:** Family Law Amendment Act 2023 commenced **6 May 2024** — repealed the equal-shared-parental-responsibility (ESPR) presumption + equal-time pathway; simplified s60CC best-interests. **"Goode v Goode" and "ESPR/equal time" are outdated.**
> - **Property:** Family Law Amendment Act 2024 commenced **10 June 2025** — codified the 4-step into the Act, made *economic effect of family violence* an express factor, moved disclosure duty into the Act (s71B married / s90RI de facto), added a companion-animals (pets) framework.

## 1. Conference workflow (ordered phases)
0. **Pre-conference:** conflict check (ASCR rr10–12), client identification/verification (anti-fraud; mandatory in conveyancing), intake (matter type, parties, objectives, urgency, limitation dates), open file.
1. **Opening:** rapport, confirm identity/authority, set agenda + purpose, **costs disclosure** (LPUL s174 — "when or as soon as reasonably practicable after instructions are given", in writing).
2. **Information-gathering:** client's account in their words, chronology of facts, identify/collect documents, clarify objectives (defines retainer scope = duty of care).
3. **Advice:** legal position, **options**, **risks of each**, alternatives to litigation (ASCR r7 — clear/timely advice + must inform of alternatives to fully-contested adjudication).
4. **Instructions:** what the client decides/authorises (ASCR r8).
5. **Next steps:** action items, who does what, timeframes; flag out-of-scope work + referrals.
6. **Closing/post:** write the **file note** contemporaneously, send confirming/engagement letter + costs agreement, calendar follow-ups + limitation dates.

## 2. Mandatory / expected documents (with obligation)
| Document | Obligation | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Costs disclosure** (basis + single estimate ex GST/disb + 4 client-rights statements) | LPUL **s174**; mandatory where est. costs > **$750**; standard form usable $750–$3,000 | **Mandatory** |
| **Costs agreement / retainer** | LPUL s180 (written/evidenced in writing); conditional = signed (s181) | Standard |
| **Conflict-check record** | ASCR rr10–12 | Compliance mandatory; record = best practice |
| **Client ID record** | VOI / gatekeeper duty | Mandatory (conveyancing), strong best practice generally |
| **File note** (advice given + instructions) | Professional standard; courts draw adverse inferences if absent | Effectively expected |
| **Engagement/confirming letter** | Defines scope/duty of care | Best practice "every matter" |

**Why costs disclosure is the highest-risk artefact:** LPUL **s178** — non-compliance ⇒ costs agreement **void**, fees **unrecoverable until assessed**, + possible **disciplinary action**. The product MUST get this right or it creates liability rather than removing it.

## 3. Defensible file note — the 10 elements (force-capture each)
accurate + comprehensive + **contemporaneous**. Record: (1) date/**time/duration**, (2) **who** present + capacity, (3) **what the client said**/instructions, (4) **advice given** (substance, not "advised client"), (5) **options** presented (incl. alternatives to litigation), (6) **risks warned of**, (7) **client's response** — esp. any decision **not** to follow advice, (8) **instructions** received, (9) **next steps** + owner + timeframe, (10) **costs discussed**/disclosed + client acknowledgement. Plain language, proportionate to complexity.
**Controlling authority:** *Sewell v Zelden* [2010] NSWSC 1180 — no written record ⇒ court preferred the client's account; failure to note advice "particularly where relevant to a conflict or where the client indicated they did not wish to follow advice" was "extraordinary … sufficient to induce doubt whether the advice was given at all."

## 4. Family-law specifics
**Two tracks** — gate separately:
- **Parenting:** best interests paramount; **current s60CC factors (post-6-May-2024)** = safety (incl. FV/abuse/neglect history) · child's views · developmental/psychological/emotional/cultural needs · each carer's capacity · benefit of relationships *where safe* · anything else; + extra ATSI cultural-connection consideration. **No ESPR presumption, no equal-time pathway.**
- **Property (4-step, codified from 10-Jun-2025):** identify pool (assets + liabilities + **super**) → contributions (financial/non-financial/homemaker, before/during/after) → current & future circumstances (former s75(2) + **economic effect of family violence**) → **just & equitable** (court not required to make an order). Plus **companion animals** framework (no joint/shared-possession orders).

**Mandatory screening / process gates (most likely to be missed — the AI must check each):**
- **Family-violence & risk screening** (s4AB definition incl. coercive control + economic abuse); court runs **Lighthouse / Family DOORS** triage.
- **Full & frank financial disclosure** — now statutory **s71B / s90RI**, ongoing, undertaking required, breach = contempt/costs.
- **Pre-action procedures** — "genuine steps", Notice of Intention (14 days), **Genuine Steps Certificate**; non-compliance ⇒ costs.
- **s60I / FDR (mediation)** — certificate required before a parenting application (5 certificate types); exemptions: urgency, FV/abuse or risk, incapacity, recent contravention.
- **Urgency triggers** (bypass gates): FV/AVO, child recovery, urgent property/injunction.
- **Time limits:** property/maintenance — **12 months post-divorce** (married) / **2 years post-separation** (de facto).

## 5. Product-build implications (the spec)
- **Costs-disclosure module** with the $750/$3,000 threshold logic + s174 content checklist — highest priority.
- **File-note template hard-requires the 10 elements**, timestamps creation (contemporaneity), and specifically prompts *risks warned of* + *client response where advice declined*.
- **Conflict-check + client-ID** generated as discrete dated retained artefacts (evidence trail).
- **Family-law knowledge base must be updated to post-2024/2025 law**; the AI should detect + flag the process gates (s60I, pre-action, disclosure, FV screening, time limits) and the two tracks.
- **Mom-Test additions:** ask how they currently produce file notes/costs disclosure; whether a tool that drafts the *costs disclosure + defensible file note* (not just "AI notes") would matter.
