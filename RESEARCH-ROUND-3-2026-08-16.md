- **UPL is an objective, per-conversation test** (LSNSW PSU Aug 2025; *Abboud* [2025] NSWCATOD 34): "whether a person … has expressly told clients that they are not legal practitioner has no bearing." Regulators list "AI / technological tools" as an emerging unqualified-practice category. LPUL s 10(2): if any part is legal practice, every $149 is repayable regardless of refund policy.
