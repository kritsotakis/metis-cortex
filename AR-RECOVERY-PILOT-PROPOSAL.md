## What we'll do

For 90 days, Metis Cortex installs and runs an automated Accounts Receivable recovery system in your practice.

The system chases your aged invoices on autopilot — same logic your team would apply manually, but consistent, persistent, and in your voice. Email + SMS sequences keyed to invoice age, with a payment link baked in.

**What gets automated:**

1. Pull aged AR from Xero (or your practice management tool)
2. Tier overdue invoices by age: T+7, T+14, T+21, T+30, T+45, T+60+
3. Send tone-appropriate reminders at each tier — drafted in your firm's voice
4. Include one-tap Stripe / EzyCollect payment link in every reminder
5. Escalate stubborn invoices to a phone-task in your CRM at T+30 (you still pick up the phone for the hard ones)
6. Real-time AR ageing dashboard you can check anytime
7. Weekly summary email: who paid, who's still outstanding, who needs personal escalation

**What it does NOT do:**
- Replace your judgement on tricky clients
- Touch financial advisory or tax prep work
- Make refund / write-off / payment-plan decisions without your approval
- Send anything without you having approved the templates upfront
