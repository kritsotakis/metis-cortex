# Positioning draft — "the paralegal suburban firms can't hire"
## FOR PETER'S REVIEW — not deployed anywhere

Source: John's own words at the 2026-08-10 demo. He is the target customer
describing his own problem; this draft just plays it back.

### The pitch (solicitor side)
**Headline:** The paralegal you can't hire.
**Sub:** Every paralegal wants to work in the city. Metis sits in your
conference, writes the file note with the two fields that decide negligence
claims, drafts the costs disclosure at the right LPUL tier, and files it all —
under your review, on your letterhead, before the client leaves the room.

### Why it works on this audience
- It names THEIR stated problem (recruitment), not our technology.
- It sets the right expectation: paralegal work, solicitor judgement. That is
  also the honest description of what the product does — and it is why an
  AI-sceptic liked it ("it always goes to a solicitor").
- "Office manager" is the buyer in a small firm. The pitch is about workflow
  hours saved, not legal reasoning.

### Where it would go (when approved)
- Home.tsx solicitor section headline + the "coming soon" blurb
- The office-manager meeting opener
- Any LEAP marketplace listing description later

- "Replaces a paralegal" for legal judgement tasks — it does document work
- "Live AustLII research" — retired claim
