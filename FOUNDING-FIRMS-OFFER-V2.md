## How recordings are handled (the boring but load-bearing part)

Before we talk price, three things you should know about what happens to client conference recordings inside Metis:

1. **Your data is never used to train AI models.** Period. Other clients' matters are not visible across tenants; your matter data stays in your tenant.
2. **Recordings are encrypted at rest in your tenant. Client withdrawal of consent triggers deletion within 7 days.** No copies retained; audit log of deletion preserved.
3. **The standard subpoena posture mirrors your existing matter records.** Metis recordings are subject to the same discovery rules as any other matter document. We do not assert privilege we don't have; we don't take possession of your client's privilege.

The all-party-consent workflow built for NSW is the first step of every matter — timestamped, audit-trailed, separately stored from the recording, defensible at compliance review.
