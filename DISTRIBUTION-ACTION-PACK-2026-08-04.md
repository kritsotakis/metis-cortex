## 2. Community answering — r/AusLegal and friends

Your exact buyer posts daily: "what documents do I need for [X]?" The 26 guides are purpose-built
answers. **Rules of engagement:** answer the question genuinely and fully in the comment itself,
link the matching guide only as a "longer version here", never link the paid product directly,
disclose it's your site. Reddit torches stealth marketing and rewards genuine help.

- r/AusLegal (~300k members) — daily family-law/tenancy/employment/fines questions
- r/AusFinance — bankruptcy/debt/Centrelink threads
- Facebook groups: "Family Law Support Australia"-type groups (several 10k+ member groups)
- Whirlpool forums — legal section, older demographic, high trust

**Sustainable pace:** 2-3 genuinely helpful answers a week beats 20 spammy ones. This can be a
Cowork-lane recurring task: find the week's matching threads, draft answers, Peter approves + posts.
