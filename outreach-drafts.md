## 0. THE CALL — primary channel, replaces cold email as the opener

Ring the firm's public number from the Law Society of NSW directory. Business
hours, and avoid Monday morning and Friday afternoon.

### Getting past reception

> "Morning — could I speak with [First name] if they're about? It's Peter
> Kritsotakis. It's not a client matter, I'm doing some research with family-law

If asked what it's regarding: *"I'm interviewing family-law solicitors about how
file notes actually get done after a client conference. Fifteen minutes, no
sales."* If they're unavailable, ask when is a better time to ring back — **do
not** leave it as "can you get them to call me."

### The call itself — 40 seconds, then stop talking

> "Thanks for taking the call. I'll be quick and I'm not selling anything.
>
> I'm talking to about ten family-law solicitors around NSW at the moment about
> one specific thing: what actually happens between finishing a client
> conference and having the file note written up. The after-hours part of it
> mostly.
>
> Could I borrow fifteen minutes some time this week or next? I'll send you what
> I learn across all the interviews either way."

**Then be quiet and let them answer.** Do not fill the silence with the product.

### Mom-Test discipline on the call — unchanged

Ask about what already happened, never about the idea:

- "When was the last new-client conference you ran?"
- "How long after did the file note actually get written up?"
- "What do you do when a client later says you never told them something?"
- "Walk me through what you do today" — never "would you use..."

**Do not describe Metis unless they ask twice.** If they do ask:
*"It's a document-organiser — it doesn't give advice, and anything it drafts
goes to the solicitor to approve. But I'd rather hear how you work first,
otherwise I'll just bias the answers."*

**Say the email is coming.** That one sentence is what stops it reading as spam

## Spam Act 2003 compliance check (email versions only — LinkedIn DMs exempt)

The Spam Act 2003 (Cth) requires three things for commercial electronic messages:

1. **Consent** — for cold B2B research outreach to publicly-listed firm addresses, "inferred consent" applies (the firm has conspicuously published the email and the message is reasonably related to their business). Templates above stay inside this.
2. **Identification** — accurate sender ID, legal entity name, and ABN. (Note: a physical postal address is a US CAN-SPAM requirement, NOT an AU Spam Act 2003 requirement, so it's omitted.) All email templates above include: Peter Kritsotakis · Kritsotakis Investments Pty Ltd ATF Kritsotakis Family Trust · ABN 45 984 876 899 · contactable email + mobile.
3. **Unsubscribe** — functional opt-out mechanism on every commercial email. All email templates above include a "reply 'no thanks'" opt-out line. Peter must actually honour replies (don't email anyone who opts out, ever).

For full safety: maintain a 1-line opt-out log alongside the tracker. If anyone replies "no thanks" / "unsubscribe" / "remove", flag the row and DO NOT send follow-ups.
